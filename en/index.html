App

FormLog

An app for tracking calories, macros and workouts in one place, with no account required. Your records are stored on your device.

  • Calorie and macro tracking: daily entries, protein–carb–fat breakdown and targets you set yourself.
  • Your own foods and meals: save the foods you use often and your regular meals.
  • Workout programs: build a weekly program and check off the exercises you complete.

FormLog is free, with no ads and no subscription. It is not yet available in app stores; store links will be added here once it is released.
