FormLog support

Short help for common questions. If you can’t find an answer, email us.

Contact

Email: faroverhealth@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Device model (e.g. iPhone 15, Samsung Galaxy S23)
  • Operating system version (e.g. iOS 26.1, Android 16)
  • FormLog version (Settings → About and support)
  • A short description of what you did and what happened

Please don’t send your backup password, your backup file, or sensitive personal information such as your weight, health or food records. We don’t need them to help you.

Adding foods

  • Log a food for the day: on the Today screen tap + Add, search for the food and enter the amount.
  • Create your own food: in the Foods tab tap + Create → Create food. You can enter the values from the package label.
  • Saved meals: save a meal you eat often with + Create → Create saved meal and add it with one tap.

Built-in food values come with the app and are approximate; no internet connection is needed.

Targets

  • Tap Set target on the Today screen or Settings → Targets, and enter your calorie and macro targets.
  • The Calorie and macro guide shows a starting estimate based on what you enter. It is an estimate, not medical advice. The age, height, weight and other details you enter in the guide are not saved; only the target values you save are stored.

Workouts

  • In the Workout tab, tap Create program to build a weekly program, or start from one of the sample programs.
  • After choosing an active program, you can check off each day’s exercises.

Backup and restore

Your records are stored only on your device and are not included in automatic iCloud/Google backups. To keep your records when you delete the app or change phones, create a backup file.

Create a backup

  1. Settings → Data → Create backup.
  2. Choose a password of at least 8 characters and enter it again.
  3. Choose where to save the file (e.g. Files on iPhone, a folder on Android).

Don’t forget your password. It is not stored anywhere and is never sent to us. If you forget it, it cannot be recovered and the backup cannot be opened.

Restore

  1. Settings → Data → Restore from backup.
  2. Choose the backup file and enter its password.
  3. Review the summary shown and confirm.

Restoring replaces your current records. The records on the device are deleted and the records in the backup are loaded; nothing is merged. If you’re unsure, back up your current data first.

Deleting your data

Settings → Data → Delete personal data permanently deletes your records from this device. Preferences such as theme and language are kept. Backup files you created stay where you saved them; you delete those yourself. Details: Privacy policy.
