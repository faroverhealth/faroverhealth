FormLog privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Applies to: FormLog 0.7.5 (iOS and Android)

Who is responsible and how to contact us

FormLog is developed by independent developer Emre Dama and published under the name “Far Over Health”. Far Over Health is a brand name, not a separate company. “We” in this policy means Emre Dama.

Privacy questions and requests: faroverhealth@gmail.com

In short

  • FormLog has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The records you enter are stored on your device. The app does not send these records to us or anyone else; it has no server connection for this purpose.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service.
  • Your records leave the device only in an encrypted backup file that you create and choose to save or share.

Data stored on your device and why

The following information is stored only in the app’s database on your device, to provide the app’s features:

  • Daily food entries, amounts and day notes
  • Foods and saved meals you create, and favorite marks
  • Calorie and macro targets
  • Workout programs, exercises and the workout history you check off
  • Preferences: theme, language, break-game sound and duration settings

Calorie and macro guide: the age, sex, height, weight and activity details you enter in the guide are used only to calculate an estimate on screen; they are not saved or sent. Only the values you choose to save as targets are stored.

The app’s database is set up to be excluded from iCloud and iPhone backups and from Android’s automatic app backup. Records on the device are not separately encrypted by the app; they are protected by your device’s operating system.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or Apple Health / Health Connect data.

Content included with the app

The built-in food database (based on USDA FoodData Central), explainer videos and game sounds are included in the app. Nothing is downloaded from the internet to show them. The app does not update its own content or code over the internet; updates come through the app store.

Encrypted backup files

  • A backup file is created only when you choose Create backup. Its contents are encrypted on your device with the password you set (scrypt + AES-256-GCM).
  • The password is not stored anywhere and is never sent to us. If you forget it, it cannot be recovered.
  • You decide where the file goes: on iPhone through the share sheet (e.g. Files, iCloud Drive, email), on Android to a folder you choose. If you choose a service such as cloud storage or email, the file is kept under that service’s terms and privacy policy. This is not something the app sends automatically.
  • The copy created in the app’s temporary folder while sharing is deleted when the operation ends.
  • Restoring a backup replaces the records currently on the device; a summary is shown and your confirmation is requested first.

Retention and deletion

  • Records stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Settings → Data → Delete personal data permanently deletes your records from this device. Preferences such as theme, language and game settings, and the app’s built-in food list, are kept.
  • Removing the app from your device deletes all of its data on that device.
  • Backup files you created stay where you saved them; you delete those yourself.
  • Because your in-app records never reach us, deleting them is in your control.

Third parties

The app does not share your personal records with any third party and contains no analytics, advertising or similar third-party service (SDK) with access to your records. The app’s own code makes no network requests.

  • External links: source, license and email links in the app open in your browser or email app only when you tap them. The privacy policies of those sites and services apply.
  • App stores and operating system: Apple (App Store, TestFlight) and Google (Google Play) process data under their own policies when the app is downloaded and distributed. If you have allowed it in your device settings, these platforms may share crash and usage information with developers. This is done by the platform, not by the app’s code.

Payments

FormLog is free, with no ads and no subscription. This version has no in-app purchases and does not process payment information.

Support emails

If you email us, your email address and what you write are used only to reply to you and resolve your issue. Emails are received through Google’s Gmail service and stored under Google’s policies. To ask for your correspondence to be deleted, write to the same address. Please do not send your backup password, backup file or health records.

About health information

FormLog is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Calculations and food values are estimates.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
